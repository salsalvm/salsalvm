## About me

I am a **Senior Flutter Developer** and **Mobile Engineer** with **4+ years** of experience designing, developing, and shipping high-performance mobile applications used by millions.

At **Real11 Fantasy Sports**, I helped scale a skill-based entertainment platform to **10M+ downloads** and **100K+ daily active users** — integrating real-time Socket.IO systems, Stripe & Razorpay payments, in-app purchases, and an observability pipeline that **cut crash rates by 28%** and **lifted 30-day retention by 40%**.

I specialize in **Clean Architecture**, **MVVM**, and **BLoC**, with production experience across Firebase, REST APIs, CI/CD with GitHub Actions, and publishing to **Google Play Store** and **Apple App Store**. I also work full-stack with **Node.js**, **Express**, **MongoDB**, and **React**.

- Location: **India**
- Currently: **Freelance / Contract** · open to full-time roles
- Focus: Flutter, Kotlin, Firebase, Clean Architecture, CI/CD
